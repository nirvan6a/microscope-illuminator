# Illuminator

A 3D-printed LED illuminator that replaces the sub-stage mirror on a microscope. It provides white and blue illumination, adjustable brightness and DC power. Built to upgrade older microscopes, including those used in school labs.

## Getting started

1. Check the microscope's mirror mount and clearance before choosing or adapting a design.
2. Print the enclosure and assemble the LEDs and controls using the circuit schematic.
3. Check the supply voltage and polarity against the circuit before connecting power.
4. Fit the illuminator, select white or blue light, and adjust the brightness.

## Notes on Blue Illumination

Blue light has a shorter wavelength and thus provides a higher resolution image.

If using blue illumination to view a sample, it is recommended you first use white illumination to focus, and then switch to blue before taking a picture.

It is not recommended to view a sample with blue illumination for long periods, as this is damaging to the eyes.

After taking a picture of a blue-illuminated sample, the image can be converted to greyscale to provide a high resolution photomicrograph without any colour artefacts.
